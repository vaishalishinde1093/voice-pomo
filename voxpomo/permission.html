Let VoxPomo hear you

Voice commands need microphone access once. Chrome will ask for permission; choose Allow. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere except to Chrome's own speech service.

Things you can say once it's on:

  • start, pause, resume, reset, skip
  • set a 45 minute timer, take a 10 minute break
  • notify me halfway, how much time is left
  • set an alarm for 3:30 pm, alarm in 20 minutes
  • start stopwatch, lap, stop stopwatch
  • pin the clock, show the clock, mute
