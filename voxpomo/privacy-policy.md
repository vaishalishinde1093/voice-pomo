# Pomo – Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 27 September 2026
**Applies to:** the "Pomo" Chrome extension

Pomo is a voice-controlled timer, stopwatch and alarm clock for Google Chrome. It is designed to work entirely inside your browser. This page explains what the extension does and does not do with your data.

## Summary

- Pomo does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal information.
- Pomo has no servers and no accounts. Nothing you do in the extension is sent to the developer.
- Your microphone is used only when you turn listening on, and only to recognise voice commands.

## Microphone and voice commands

When you enable listening (by pressing the microphone button or the keyboard shortcut), the extension uses the Web Speech API built into Google Chrome to convert your speech into text. Chrome may send that audio to Google's speech recognition service to perform the transcription; this is handled by the browser under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), not by the extension.

The extension receives only the resulting text, uses it to run a command (for example "set a 25 minute timer"), and discards it. No audio or transcripts are recorded, stored or sent anywhere by the extension. Listening stays off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time.

## Data stored on your device

Pomo saves the following in Chrome's local extension storage on your own device so the clock can keep working between sessions:

- timer and stopwatch state
- alarms you have set
- your preferences (default timer length, voice and sound options, floating-clock position)

This data never leaves your browser and is removed when you uninstall the extension.

## Permissions explained

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| Access to http/https websites | To draw the floating clock over the page you are viewing. The extension does not read or modify page content. |
| Notifications | To tell you when a timer, halfway reminder or alarm goes off. |
| Alarms | To keep timers and alarms running while the popup is closed. |
| Storage | To save the state and settings described above. |
| Text-to-speech | To speak confirmations using voices installed in your browser or operating system. |
| Offscreen document | A hidden page the extension uses to run speech recognition and play chimes in the background. |

## Analytics, advertising and third parties

Pomo contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party code that reports usage. The only external service involved is Chrome's own speech recognition described above.

## Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
